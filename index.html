Do you handle both residential and commercial AV projects?

Yes, absolutely. We work with homeowners on home theater and whole house audio projects, and we also serve businesses including restaurants, retail stores, corporate offices, and boardrooms across Long Island, NY.

What is included in a conference room AV installation?

A typical conference room AV installation includes display screens or video walls, cameras, microphones, speakers, control systems, and video conferencing integration. We design each system to match the size and needs of your specific meeting space.

Yes, we specialize in retrofitting existing rooms with full home theater systems. We assess the acoustics, lighting, and layout of your space and design a custom solution that includes surround sound, projector or large-screen TV, seating recommendations, and smart controls.
